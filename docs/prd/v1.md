# PRD v1: Lofi Lobby

## Problem

Lo-fi streams are a nice background for studying or working, but they are passive: you can't see who else is there or say hi. Discord voice rooms are the opposite: too much. There's nothing in between that feels like a calm place you share with other people.

## Who it's for

The community around a dev YouTube channel: people who study or code for hours and like having company in the background.

## Goals

- Open a link and be **in a cozy town at night**, with no sign-up.
- **See other people** walking around and **talk by text**, with the message over your character.
- **Everyone hears the same song at the same second**, picked by the system, like a radio.
- The owner can **restart or skip** the song for the whole room.
- Run on a free Cloudflare account, with **no server to maintain**.

## Out of scope (v1)

- Accounts, private rooms with owners, custom scenes.
- Moderation tools (mute, ban, word filter) and chat history.
- Choosing songs by vote.

## Success

People leave the tab open for hours and come back to it.
